Privacy Policy

What we collect

  • Account: email, password (stored hashed), display name, and optional username, phone and profile picture. Your birth year is used only to confirm you are 21 or older.
  • Nicotine and health information you enter: which products you use, how often, what you spend, your reasons for using or quitting, quit-plan settings, daily logs and cravings, and optional health conditions and U.S. state. This is sensitive information. You choose what to answer. Health conditions and state have a "Prefer not to say" option.
  • What you do in the app: products you scan, search or add, ratings, reviews, community posts and comments, votes, reports and blocks. Pack photos you choose to upload.
  • Reports: if you report a post, comment or review, we keep your reason and a copy of up to 300 characters of the reported text so staff can review it.
  • Not collected: precise location, contacts, advertising identifiers. We do not use third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.

Why we use it

  • To run the app: show your history, sync your quit tracker across your phones, and personalize ratings, warnings and craving tips.
  • To keep the community safe and enforce our rules.
  • To improve product data. Products you add are stored without your name.
  • Research (off unless you turn it on): if you opt in, anonymous answers may be counted in combined results. Results are reviewed only for groups of 10 or more people. You can turn this off in Profile at any time.

What we never do

  • We never sell your data and never use it for advertising or marketing.
  • We never share your nicotine-use or health information with nicotine brands, retailers, advertisers or data brokers.
  • Brands cannot pay to change a score or to see your information.

Who processes it for us

Our hosting and database providers, only to run Leafcheck for us. If you use Talk to AI, your question and the source text you are asking about are sent to our AI provider to produce an answer. We do not send your name, email, or tracker and health answers with it. We share information when the law requires it or to protect people from harm.

Your choices and rights

  • Access and download: Profile > Download my data.
  • Delete: Profile > Delete my account and data. We delete your account data from live systems right away, and backups are overwritten within 35 days.
  • Withdraw consent: turn off research sharing in Profile, or delete your account. Withdrawing health-data consent means the quit tracker and personalization stop working.
  • Residents of Washington, California and other states may have additional rights, including to know what we hold, correct it, delete it, and appeal a refusal. You may also use an authorized agent. Email [email protected] and we will respond within 45 days. We will not treat you differently for using these rights.

Retention and security

We keep your data until you delete your account. Data is encrypted in transit. If there is a security breach affecting your information, we will notify you and regulators as the law requires.

Sale, sharing and advertising

We do not sell personal information, we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, and we do not use it to profile you for ads. We do not respond to "Do Not Track" signals because we do not track you across other sites or apps.

Sensitive information

Health and nicotine-use information is sensitive. We use it only to provide the features you asked for, which is why you give a separate consent for it. Our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy is a separate page in Profile and on the welcome screen.

Adults only, United States only

Leafcheck is for people 21 and older in the United States. We ask for your birth month and year, and we do not create accounts for anyone under 21. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 21, and we delete it if we learn we have. Parents can email [email protected].

Changes and contact

We will tell you in the app before material changes take effect. Questions or requests: [email protected]. Effective October 16, 2026.
