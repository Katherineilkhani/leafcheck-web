Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy. Effective October 16, 2026. This policy covers only consumer health data, as Washington's My Health My Data Act and similar laws define it.

Categories of consumer health data we collect, and why

  • Nicotine and tobacco product use: the products you use, how much and how often, first use after waking, spending, past quit attempts, and your readiness to cut back or quit.
  • Quit plan and tracker data: your plan, daily counts, cravings, moods and notes.
  • Health conditions you choose to share: for example pregnancy, heart or lung conditions, or mental health. Optional. You can pick "Prefer not to say".
  • Your reasons for quitting or using, and your U.S. state.

Purpose: to personalize product ratings and warnings, build your quit plan and craving tips, estimate your nicotine intake, and sync these across your devices. We do not use it for advertising or marketing.

Categories of sources

Only you, by typing or tapping answers in the app. We do not buy or receive consumer health data from anyone else.

Categories of consumer health data we share

None. We do not sell it, and we do not share it with third parties or affiliates. Our hosting and database providers store it for us under contract and may not use it for their own purposes. If you turn on the optional research setting, only combined results for groups of 10 or more people are produced, with no names or emails.

Categories of third parties and affiliates we share with

None.

How to exercise your rights

  • Access: Profile > Download my data.
  • Withdraw consent: Profile > Withdraw health-data consent. This deletes your nicotine-use, quit-tracker and health answers.
  • Delete: Profile > Delete my account and data. Live copies are deleted right away and backups are overwritten within 35 days.
  • Questions, requests or an appeal if we refuse: email [email protected]. We respond within 45 days.
